It starts with the business problem, not the chart

These six questions come back on every dataset. They aren't a checklist. Each answer sends me back to the one before it.

  • What problem are we actually trying to solve?
  • What data do we have, and what does it tell us?
  • What patterns, trends or anomalies exist?
  • What factors may be influencing the outcome?
  • Which insights matter most to stakeholders?
  • What action can be taken based on the Results?
